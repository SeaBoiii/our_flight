# frame.md: Golden Hour Departure (trailer)

Brand truth for *Your Seat Is Waiting*. It is derived from the invitation site's `DESIGN_LANGUAGE.md`.

## Concept angle
A guest's journey from midnight to golden hour, filmed as a premium airline title sequence. Paper, foil and light tell the story; type lands on the music.

## Palette
- **Background:** midnight `#04121B`; navy-deep `#052433`; brand navy `#033A4E`, sampled from the A&N monogram.
- **Foreground:** ivory `#F8F3EA`; paper `#FFFDF8`.
- **Accent:** champagne gold `#D9B472`, sampled from the monogram. Soft gold is `#EAD3A2`; deep gold `#A9834A` is for text on light surfaces.
- **Golden-hour ramp (light only, never text):** `#F6DCBC`, `#EFBF90`, `#E3A37C`.
- One accent hue (gold). Neutrals are tinted navy, never pure black or white.
- Dark scenes use solid or radial fills only: no full-screen linear gradients, because they band under H.264.

## Typography
- **Display:** Instrument Serif 400 and italic (self-hosted woff2). Names, title cards, hero lines. Tracking −0.02 to −0.04em.
- **Data and labels:** IBM Plex Mono 500 (self-hosted). Flight codes, boards, dates, seat numbers, uppercase with +0.16em tracking.
- **Captions:** Instrument Serif italic at 46–54px, ivory on a soft navy scrim. One sentence at a time, lower third, inside the 9:16 safe zones.
- **Arabic:** none in the trailer.

## Materials
- Cotton-paper texture (`assets/art/cotton-paper.png`) on paper surfaces: pass, card, envelope.
- Gold foil on ivory uses the ink-foil gradient: it rests in deep gold, and the highlight only sweeps across.
- Songket *pucuk rebung*: a fine gold border motif, used once per scene at most.
- The A&N monogram is `assets/brand/monogram-a-and-n.png`. Never redraw or recolour its lettering.
- The QR code is `assets/brand/rsvp-qr.svg`, always on ivory paper with a quiet zone.

## Motion
- Scroll-free cinema: slow camera pushes (Ken Burns 3–6%), light leaks on emotional turns, title cards on bar lines (112.3 BPM, bar = 2.14s).
- Eases: expo.out and power3.out for arrivals, power2.in for exits. No bounce, except the boarding stamp's physical overshoot.
- Constant fine film grain over everything; a subtle letterbox breathe during the take-off.

## Do / Don't
- Do: let images breathe; one hero element per frame; keep the guest faceless (hands or silhouette only).
- Don't: show any invitation code; show both dates in one cut; use neon, purple gradients or generic stock-wedding florals.
